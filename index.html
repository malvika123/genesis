Genesis v0.6

Every creature is a reinforcement-learning agent with its own personality. Its eyes report what lies in eight directions, its body reports hunger, thirst, tiredness and pain, and a neural network chooses what to do: walk, stay, eat, drink, sleep, mate, attack, or share food. Survival is wired in like a brainstem: when starving it reaches for food in front of it, when parched it drinks. Everything else is learned from what its body feels. It can only fall asleep when tired, then sleeps until rested or woken by pain. Children can’t mate: people reach that at 16 (hunter-gatherers have their first child around 19 to 20), predators at 4, herbivores at 1.5. A child gets a random mix of its parents’ wiring, and only habits a parent learned strongly can pass on, and only partly.

Human minds differ from the animals in the ways research points to: a bigger brain, a long childhood and a learning window that stays open well into adulthood (animals stop learning as much once grown), a strong curiosity that rewards finding new places, imitation of elders, and a long memory of where food and water were. Fish live in the ponds and eat algae. People can learn to catch them, and predators hunt anything, including each other when starving.

Pink is female, blue is male, yellow-green ovals are herbivores, red diamonds are predators, white fish swim in the water, faded means asleep. Rivers and ponds have shallow edges for drinking and a darker deep middle that can drown you. Black holes are pits that trap and hurt. Red dots on grass are poisonous berries. One cell is about 3 km, one step is 15 minutes, and a year is squeezed into 3 days. Click a creature to see its thoughts.

Whisper an idea

Click a creature and type anything. It reaches them in their sleep or a quiet moment, as their own thought. Your words are read for the ideas in them (water, food, fish, berries, hunters, pits, sharing, others, sleep, a partner, exploring, a direction) and whether you say do or don’t. Each becomes a small nudge in their brain, never a command. Words that mean nothing in their world stay a vague feeling.
