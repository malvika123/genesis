Why: at realistic speed (x4) predators kill about 125 per 1,000 hominin-years (8 times the x1 rate); 40% of the victims were asleep, none up a tree; only 6-7% of sleeping hominins are up a tree, and only 3% of 250 m valley squares reach the 0.4 tree cover a hominin needs to climb (a threshold set for the globe's 3 km squares). Real savanna chimpanzees nest in trees on about 88-97% of nights (ground nests 3-12%; second-hand figures, 24 Nov search); Lucy's anatomy suggests some tree use (debated).
