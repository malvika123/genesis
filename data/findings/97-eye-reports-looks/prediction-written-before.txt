Entry 97: the eye reports what a thing looks like, not how much the body needs it (Fable, 17 Nov 2026, labelled-senses review).
Written 17 Nov 2026, before the change is built or run.

The rule today: in src/senses.js sense(), the food and water channels (and, for hunters and fish-eating hominins, the prey and fish
channels) are multiplied by (0.15 + hunger) or (0.15 + thirst). A full creature sees food at 15-20% strength, a starving one at full
strength or more. No eye does this; hunger already reaches the brain by its own inputs (x[IB], x[IB+1]).
The change: with the switch (--no-need-weighted-eye, name to be confirmed in the code) those channels report only how near and how clear
the thing is: strength = nearness, whatever the hunger. The default stays the old rule until the arms are measured. This applies to every kind
(hominins, plant-eaters, hunters, fish): it is what the eye does, not a hominin-only rule.

Test: 24 worlds x 100 days, seeds 1-24, default settings, 4 workers, nice; old rule against the switch; paired by seed. The identical-worlds
check (default against the commit before) must say IDENTICAL. Both arms start from the same founders; worlds will differ at once by chance.

What I expect, and why:
1. The brains were trained (within one life, by reinforcement) with a food signal that rose with hunger. Without it, the food channel is
   always on at full nearness, so a full creature is pulled toward food as strongly as a hungry one until its brain learns to gate
   the pull by its own hunger input. So I expect MORE time walking toward and standing at food when full, and a little more wandering:
   hominin energy at day 100 a few points lower, hunger deaths per hominin-year up, by between 10% and 60% (the range is wide on purpose:
   I do not know how much the brain's own hunger input already carries).
2. Hunters see prey at full strength when full: attacks while full rise from the satiety-brake level (entries 92-94) toward the old
   no-brake level; kills per hunter per day up a little. Plant-eaters: no clear change, since a grazer is rarely full for long.
3. Water: thirst deaths up by a similar amount in worlds with dry stretches.
4. Discovery firsts, births and the sex shares of entry 95 do not move by more than seed noise.
5. If nothing about hunger deaths moves (within +-10%), that would mean the brain's own hunger input carried the behaviour and the
   eye's multiplier was decoration. That is a result too, and the arm is then kept as the honest rule.

Not held if: hunger deaths fall by more than 10%, or rise by more than 60%; or hunters' attacks while full do not rise.
Strength warning: 24 worlds with seed-to-seed spread; a change under about 20% in a rate of rare deaths will be called a trace, "weak".
Decision rule before the run (no tuning toward an outcome): the honest rule is the realism rule. It is adopted as the default unless it
makes the world collapse (people extinct in more than 12 of 24 worlds), in which case something real is missing (the owner's rule) and
I stop and ask for Opus and Fable rather than adjust numbers.
