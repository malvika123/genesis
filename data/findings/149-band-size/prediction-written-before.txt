Prediction (26 Nov 2026, before the runs). Band size at the start: --people 25 (control; entry 148 'on' arm), 60, 100 (chimpanzee communities about 30-80, Ngogo near 200; foragers' bands 20-40), with --valley-prey-scale, dens-tau 1, --hom-avoid, --no-big-attacks, valley x1, 3 worlds (seeds 1-3) x 5 years.
Reasoning (back of the envelope, not measured): in real life a leopard's take of primates spread over a primate population gives a per-animal risk that falls as the population is larger; the sim's hunters are placed at a fixed density around the band, so if their take on hominins stays about the same per hunter, the risk per person should fall with band size. But a comparison of leopard predation across primates found rates were not lower in bigger groups (positive with group size, density the best predictor), so the other outcome is possible.
Expect (per person-day, predator deaths): 25: about 1-2% (entries 143-148); 60: about 0.5-1%; 100: about 0.3-0.7%.
Wrong if: the per-person rate does not fall by at least 30% from 25 to 100 (then hunters' take scales with the number of people, as in the leopard comparison), or rises.
