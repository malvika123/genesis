Written 13 Nov 2026 BEFORE the runs (commit in ./commit). Validation of tools/analysis/spread.js (second review, finding 2).
A planted pretend behaviour moves with the real hominins in 8 worlds x 60 days: picked up alone with chance 0.00002 a step, and
- POSITIVE control (--plant=0.002): an extra 0.002 a step once a hominin has watched a holder do it (spreads by watching);
- NEGATIVE control (--plant=0): watching does nothing.
What the tool must show if it is sound:
1. Positive: rate ratio (watched / never watched) clearly above 1 overall and in most worlds (expected far above: about 100x).
2. Negative: ratio about 1 overall, and above 1 in about half the worlds. A ratio clearly below or above 1 here measures the
   tool's bias (survivorship, place, founders), which then must be allowed for when reading nuts and stick digging.
Also recorded: the same real behaviours with --born-only (only hominins born during the run at risk).
