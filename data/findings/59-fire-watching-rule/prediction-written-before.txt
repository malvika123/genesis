12 identical worlds x 60 days, 25 and 80 founders, with fire watching on (default) and off (--no-fire-watching).
Primary outcome: for fire-related firsts (picking up a burning stick, eating wildfire-cooked food, making fire by rubbing, starting
a fire with a burning stick), in how many worlds the 80-founder band was first, and by how many days.
1. With fire watching ON: firsts sooner with 80 founders in most worlds (as entry 53).
2. With it OFF: still sooner with 80, but less so for making fire by rubbing (the skill the rule spreads); picking up a burning stick
   and eating cooked food depend on wildfires and numbers, so stay about as before.
3. Fewer worlds make fire by rubbing with it off.
