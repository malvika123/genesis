Entry 92, the satiety brake (Fable's review of 17 Nov, option 2 keyed to the stomach). Written before the run with the brake.
An inborn pull against attacking that grows with stomach fill: RF[A_ATK] -= SOC.fullBrake x stomach fill x (prey beside), with
SOC.fullBrake = 12, the hungry pull's full size (5 + 7), borrowed as the prey's flight pull borrowed the hominins'; tested at 6 and 24.
--no-full-brake restores. Measured with tools/analysis/hunt-measure.js, 24 worlds x 20 days, against the baseline (same worlds).
1. Identical worlds with --no-full-brake (seeds 1-2 x 20 days).
2. Attacks on a stomach over 2/3 full fall to a small share (under 10%) for all three kinds.
3. Sabre-tooth kills per adult per day fall, to about 0.4-0.6 - NOT to the field's 0.12-0.23 (if they do, suspect starving).
4. Sabre-tooth meat a day moves toward need from above; leopard meat a day does not fall.
5. No rise in hunters' starvation (energy at attack and kills hold for leopard and hyena).
If kills fall but meat falls below need, or hunters starve: stop, do not adjust.
