Written 13 Nov 2026 BEFORE the runs (commit in ./commit). 8 worlds (seeds 1-8) x 60 days, new copying and --copy-moves.
For each behaviour: rate of doing it for the first time among hominins who had watched someone do it in their square, divided by
the rate among those who never had (the rate ratio). Sharing a place raises it under both copying rules.
Primary outcome: the rate ratio for cracking nuts and for digging with a stick (the common behaviours), new against old copying.
1. With the new copying, watching raises first-time rates (ratio above 1) for nuts and stick digging.
2. The ratio is higher with the new copying than with the old (watching itself passes the behaviour on).
3. Rare behaviours (fire, meat, cutting) have too few first times to tell.
If (2) fails, discoveries are not spreading by watching, and the band-size hypothesis still has no mechanism in Genesis.
