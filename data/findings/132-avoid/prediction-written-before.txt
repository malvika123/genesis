Basis: Hayward et al.: leopard took baboons as 2.8% of kills vs 5.1% of available prey (0.55); spotted hyena 0.75% vs 26.1% (0.03). Taï (Boesch 1991): leopards attacked chimpanzees 9 times, killed 4 (44% of attacks); real attack rate about 0.30 per chimpanzee per year; death rate 0.055 per year (median primates about 0.03).
Counting run: 12 worlds (seeds 1-12), 40 days each, real densities, cast of 127. Arms: R (no avoid) and RH (--hom-avoid). Count every hunter attack with a hominin in reach; divide by hominin-years lived.
