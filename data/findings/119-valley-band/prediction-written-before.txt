One run of each: no scaling of hunters or prey to the valley yet (the valley holds the same default animals: 20 herbivore groups' worth, 6 hunters, 24 fish, in 3,000 km2 instead of the globe's 90,000 km2: hunter and prey DENSITY per km2 is about 30 times higher than the globe's in the valley, since the animal counts are per world).

Prediction: valley deaths by predators per 1,000 hominin-years higher than the globe's 27 (entry 113): 30-60; hunger deaths similar or lower (food per square per 250 m is the globe's per-square amount at 144 times the density, i.e. about 10 person-days per square: food is abundant); births about 44 per 1,000 hominin-years as on the globe; net change a year between -4% and 0; worlds emptied 3-8 of 12 (more than the globe's 5 of 24).
Not held if: predator deaths in the valley are below the globe's (then the animals are not meeting the band: ask Opus about hunter placement); or hunger deaths above 20 per 1,000 hominin-years (food in the valley is not abundant); or more than 8 of 12 emptied (stop rule: ask).
Reading: this is only the first look: the valley's animals are not yet scaled to its area (V2g). If predation is much higher than 3% a year it is the animal counts, not the valley, and the fix is hunters by area at field density (Carbone & Gittleman 2002), not a change to predation.
