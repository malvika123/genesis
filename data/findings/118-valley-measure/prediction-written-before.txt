Entry 118 - V2-0: the valley and the globe measured in metres (written 20 Nov 2026 BEFORE the run; Opus's V2 design, docs/design/valley-spec.md). Measuring only: tools/analysis/valley-measure.js. Globe and valley, 4 worlds each (seeds 1-4), 30 days (10 years), default settings, one band of 25; positions sampled every hour (4 steps). Metres per square: globe 3000, valley 250 (the sim's own conventions: SQM).
Quantities (per world, pooled): nearest-neighbour distance between people; party size (connected groups, people linked when within 1 square); mother-juvenile distance (children under 5 years, by their mother); distance moved per day per person; the band's extent after 30 days (the standard deviation of people's positions; the extent in metres); steps with nobody else within 4 squares ('alone'); lightning strikes per km2 per year and fires started.
Prediction (Opus): in SQUARE units the valley and the globe behave alike (same rules); in METRES the valley band is about 12 times smaller in everything the globe measures in squares, unless something else drives it. The smoke run showed the valley band spreading from 5 to 48 squares in 40 days; I expect the globe's band spread to stay within about 6-10 squares (a globe band ranges about 5.6 squares a year: BACKLOG), so the valley's spread in squares is several times the globe's: the sign that cover (sight) and novelty, not units, drive it. Expected in the valley (squares): nearest-neighbour distance 3-10 squares; share of steps 'alone' much higher than on the globe; mother-juvenile distance within 1-3 squares; lightning strikes per km2 about 100-150 times the globe's (rolled per square).
Not held if: the valley's spread in squares is within a factor 1.5 of the globe's (then the valley is behaving like the globe and the smoke run was a one-off); or mother-juvenile distance exceeds 5 squares (mothers and children split: a real problem); or lightning per km2 is within 20% of the globe's (the roll is not per square).
Reading: this decides which V2 step comes first (the order is Opus's: V2a the SQM constant, V2b speed with novelty, V2c sight in metres). Nothing is changed.
