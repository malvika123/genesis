Written 13 Nov 2026 BEFORE any run with hard nuts (no outcome looked at; only a 15-day smoke test of the tool, in which nobody
was yet skilled). Code: hard nut cracking (backlog A26), design revised after the checkpoint-1 review.

Numbers fixed now: beginner 0.02 per strike, expert 0.83, 500 strikes to 80% of the way, no success before 3.5 years; ends
tested: hard (beginner 0.005, 1500 strikes) and easy (0.10, 150 strikes). Skilled = skill 0.5.

Runs: 24 identical worlds x 150 days, nut-practice.js.
 central: copying on | --no-nut-copying | --no-copying
 hard end: on | --no-nut-copying        easy end: on | --no-nut-copying

MAIN OUTCOME (one): strikes on nuts per 1,000 steps with the means at hand, ages 3.5-10, hominins born after the first hominin
in the world reached skill 0.5; paired by world, "on" against "--no-nut-copying".
PASS (the reviewer's criterion): "on" higher in at least 18 of the worlds where both arms have data (scaled: 75%), median ratio
at least 1.5, and the same sign at both ends. Anything else is a clear no and will be published as one.

Predictions:
1. Main: FAIL. Ratio 1.0-1.3, higher in about half the worlds (agreeing with the reviewer).
2. Copying off: lone learners still appear (some born-in-run hominins skilled by 10) - nut cracking will still be learnable
   alone, unlike Koops et al. 2022, because every strike is practice whatever happens.
3. In some worlds nobody reaches skill 0.5 in 150 days at the hard end; there the main outcome is missing, not zero.
4. Skilled hominins born in the run will be alive after every founder has died in at least some worlds at the central setting.
