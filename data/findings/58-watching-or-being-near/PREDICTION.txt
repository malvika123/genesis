Written 13 Nov 2026 BEFORE the runs (commit in ./commit). The decisive test of learning by watching (second review, finding 2.4).
12 worlds (seeds 1-12) x 100 days; only hominins born in the run at risk; and for nuts and stick digging only in steps when they have
the means at hand (nuts and a stone; roots and a stick: --means). Copying on (default) against --no-copying (nobody copies anyone).
With --no-copying, watching cannot teach: any ratio above about 0.8 there comes from place or timing, not learning.
Primary outcome: the watched/never ratio for cracking nuts, copying on against off, per world.
1. With copying on, the ratio is higher than with copying off, in most worlds (learning by watching is real).
2. With copying off, the ratio is about 0.8-1 (place no longer explains it once the means are counted).
If (1) fails, the "young who watched start more often" of entry 57 was being near nuts, not watching.
