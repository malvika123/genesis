# Which real basin for the valley? (20 Nov 2026) — research agent, read only; abstracts and snippets: many numbers secondary or missing

**Recommended basin: Afar, Ethiopia — the Gona/Hadar/Ledi-Geraru stretch of the Awash River, about 2.6–2.5 Ma:** the earliest dated Oldowan sites, a river through the whole interval, cobble stone under the sites, and a published vegetation record. Runner-up: Omo-Turkana (Shungura Members C–E), mainly for fauna. Laetoli/Olduvai are out (wrong time or region).

**Corrections to my brief.** Braun et al. 2008, "Landscape-scale variation in hominin tool use", is in *J Hum Evol* 55:1053 and covers Koobi Fora Okote Member (Developed Oldowan, later than 2.5 Ma): not for 2.5 Ma stone distances. The Ledi-Geraru *Homo* jaw (LD 350-1) is 2.80–2.75 Ma, not 2.58 Ma (Villmoare et al. 2015, *Science* 347:1352); surrounding sediments 2.84–2.58 Ma.

**For the specification (the agent's):** stand-in = the Awash axial valley at Gona/Hadar about 2.6–2.5 Ma (a reconstruction of one real kind of place, not a surveyed map); vegetation a mosaic, about 50% grass, 30–50% woody cover as a range, densest trees and *Ficus*/*Tamarix* along the Awash, reeds and sedges at wet margins; one permanent river and seasonal pools, water scarce in the dry season (lake-margin salinity at Turkana not verified: leave out); cobble conglomerate beds near the river with sites on or beside them, rock trachyte, rhyolite, basalt, fine volcanics (no chert or quartz claim at Gona is supported), bands prefer better rock over the nearest; **scale: 750 km² is about 9–12 savanna chimpanzee ranges (63–85 km²), but at savanna densities of 0.03–0.05 per km² it holds only about 20–40 individuals in total: roughly one band of 25 (a count of 4–12 bands is the agent's inference, an assumption)**; grass-eating bovids dominate; sleeping trees in the river woodland (no density figure found).
**Not verified:** rainfall, temperature and dry-season length at 2.5 Ma; stone distances at Gona, Lokalalei and Bouri; australopith home range and group size; distances between neighbouring communities; Amboseli baboon range; Fongoli distance to water and nest-tree density; Levin et al. 2008/2011 numbers; the predator guild (Werdelin & Lewis 2013, covered in `2026-11-18-predator-guild-sources.md`); Bobe & Behrensmeyer 2004's Alcelaphini claim; Reed 1997; Bonnefille.
