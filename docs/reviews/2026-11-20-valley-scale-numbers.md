# Real-world numbers for the valley's rules in metres and minutes (20 Nov 2026) — research agent, read only; 7 searches, no full papers opened; most movement, spacing and predator numbers are from memory: LOW confidence

**One line:** for 15-minute steps and 250 m squares, a band of 25 should walk about 0.3–0.5 km per step at most (daily path 3–6 km), stay in parties of about 3–10 strung out over 20–100 m, and flee at no more than about 5 m/s.

**Recommended values (the agent's).** (1) Travel: a day's path 3–6 km (Fongoli 3.3 km as the anchor, baboons up to 8 km as the ceiling); up to about 25% of the day travelling for the band; about 0.3–0.5 km per step at most (1–2 squares), most steps zero or under a square; per-step displacement while foraging 10–60 m; hominin walking 0.6–1.3 m/s, default 1.0 m/s (900 m per step if walked continuously); net daily displacement from the sleeping site capped at about 1.5–2.5 km (inference). (2) Cohesion: parties of 3–10 (Ugalla mean 3.1: a band of 25 splits often); individuals 5–30 m apart (low); dependent infants within 1–5 m, juveniles under 5 within 10–50 m; anyone more than about 250 m away is out of the party; the whole band scattered over 100–500 m. (3) Senses: detection 30–100 m in woodland and 150–300 m in open grass; loud calls up to 1 km (working value 500 m; one square the minimum); quiet contact calls 20–50 m (estimate). (4) Mating and care: a consorting male within 5–20 m (unverified); grooming at contact; nursing mothers within 5–30 m of another adult. (5) Predators: leopard strike 5–20 m (a hominin that has not seen it by then cannot flee); flight 3–5 m/s at most (4.9 m/s the ceiling for australopiths; 6–7 m/s chimpanzees); a fleeing hominin covers a few hundred metres in a step but cannot outrun a lion or hyena over open ground: escapes are trees, groups and distance before detection. (6) Food: keep 8–12 kcal/min; patch density, time in a patch and bout length not sourced: a placeholder of one to two steps per patch, an assumption.
**Could not verify:** Ugalla and Issa daily paths; Mt Assirik; bonobos; the Fongoli party-size table and feeding and resting shares; baboon time budgets and troop spread; all predator distances and the chimpanzee sprint speed; all food-density numbers.
