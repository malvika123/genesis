## Full-text addendum (24 Nov 2026): read from PDF text (pdftotext); page numbers approximate.
- **Mille-Logya, about 2.9-2.4 Ma (Alemseged 2020, Nat Commun 11:2480, Tables 1-2):** large fauna Deinotherium bozasi, Elephas recki, Hippopotamus aff. protamphibius, Kolpochoerus limnetes, Notochoerus euilus, Giraffa, Sivatherium maurusium, Pelorovis cf. kaisensis, Ceratotherium, Diceros, Hipparionini, **Crocuta eturono, cf. Dinofelis** (so spotted hyena and the false sabre-tooth are in the Afar record at that time), Theropithecus. Comparative lists (Hata Member, Bouri about 2.5 Ma; Gurumaha-Lee Adoyta 2.8-2.5 Ma) include Eurygnathohippus afarense and E. hasumense, Metridiochoerus andrewsi, Hexaprotodon. No body-size column.
- **Abundance (Mille-Logya):** bovids by far the most common mammals, almost half of identifiable bovid specimens Alcelaphini, then Reduncini, Bovini, Aepycerotini, Antilopini, Tragelaphini; rhinos rare; equids fragmentary but quite common; Notochoerus the most common suid; Giraffa not particularly large (a smaller G. cf. gracilis also present). Faunal shift to more open conditions after about 2.9 Ma.
- **Omo / Shungura (Plummer 2015, citing Bobe & Behrensmeyer 2004):** open-grassland taxa under 15% of the large-mammal sample; open habitat about 11% and light cover 16% of the bovid sample; wetland ecomorph 5% of Member C, at most 10% of D.
- **Large carnivorans (Werdelin & Lewis 2013, read in full):** 29 of 78 species are 21.5 kg or more; bins 3.5-3, 3-2.5, 2.5-2, 2-1.5 Ma, Recent; the species-by-species coding is in a spreadsheet supplement (Table S1) NOT yet opened.
- **Biomass:** only second-hand: herbivore biomass peaks at intermediate rainfall on nutrient-rich soils, about 100 kg per km2 of metabolic biomass per zebra (Karp 2024 abstract). No primary kg per km2 table found.
- **Still NOT FOUND:** body masses for hippo, Elephas recki, hipparion, pigs, Sivatherium, Theropithecus; the Hadar Maka'amitalu Table 2 (PeerJ is behind a Cloudflare block); Bobe & Behrensmeyer 2004 and 2002 abundance shares; DiMaggio 2015 supplement; Coe 1976, East 1984, Hempson 2015.
