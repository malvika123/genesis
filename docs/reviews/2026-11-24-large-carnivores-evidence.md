# Large carnivores of eastern Africa, about 2.5 Ma (24 Nov 2026)
Research agent (6 tool calls); extends 2026-11-18-predator-guild-sources.md. Most body, group and life-history numbers are from memory and unchecked.
**Lead.** About 15 large carnivorans (21.5 kg or more) at 2.5-2.0 Ma, against 6 today (Werdelin & Lewis 2013, PMC3590191). Best supported: leopard, spotted hyena, Dinofelis. Homotherium at Hadar mostly before 3 Ma; Megantereon likely, unchecked. LIONS NOT SUPPORTED. No carnivoran list found for Gona or Ledi-Geraru; Hadar Maka'amitalu (about 2.4-1.9 Ma) carnivores rare.
**Draft sim profiles (kg; per 100 km2; own estimates unless sourced).** Leopard 30-60, ambush, alone, 3-6 (Serengeti 5.5, Allen 2020), climbs. Spotted hyena 45-65, clan 6-90, 5-10 (modern 3.5-10.8 Ruaha; 143-215 historic Serengeti), cannot climb. Giant hyena Pachycrocuta 100-190, scavenger, 1-2. Hunting hyena Chasmaporthetes 35-60, pursuit, 1-3. Megantereon 60-110, ambush, probably alone, 1-3. Homotherium 150-250, 0.5-1.5. Dinofelis 70-120, ambush, 1-2. Cheetah, wild dog, jackals, small cats, giant otter Enhydriodon, crocodiles, eagles, snakes: low confidence or unsourced. Crocodile hominin predation at Olduvai about 1.8 Ma (later than 2.5 Ma).
**Hominin predation.** No rate can be sourced. Leopard best linked (SK 54, South Africa, about 1.8-2.0 Ma); Dinofelis possible (Brain 1981); eagle and crocodile cases disputed and later. Sim: leopard, Dinofelis, Megantereon as the real risk to sleepers; hyenas as risk to small or weak and scavenger rivals; crocodiles a water danger; any rate an unknown.
**Recommendation.** Add Dinofelis and Pachycrocuta (and later Homotherium, Chasmaporthetes) behind switches; no lions.
**Not verified.** Gona/Ledi-Geraru lists; first and last East African dates for Megantereon, Chasmaporthetes, Pachycrocuta; when modern-like Crocuta appeared; first African lion; Pliocene cheetah, wild dog, small cats; pythons and eagles; densities from memory; any hominin predation rate.

## Live-checked addendum (24 Nov 2026, [O] = page opened; [S] = search snippet only). This section OVERRIDES the memory-based profiles above.
- Large carnivorans over 21.5 kg: 17 at 3.0-2.5 Ma, 15 at 2.5-2.0 Ma, 6 today [O] (Werdelin & Lewis 2013, PMC3590191, Table 1). No large canid at 3.0-2.5 Ma; at 2.5-2.0 Ma no bears, large otters present. The text names no per-species list.
- Hadar (whole formation, Wikipedia [O]): Dinofelis, Homotherium, Panthera, Chasmaporthetes, Crocuta, Ikelohyaena, Hyaena, Canis, Nyctereutes; list incomplete. Hadar Maka'amitalu about 2.35 Ma [O, first half of paper only] (PMC8994497): Dinofelis cf. aronoki, cf. Acinonyx, a hyaenid in Crocuta size range, a felid, a lutrine; Homotherium, Megantereon, Pachycrocuta, leopard, Lycaon NOT listed there. Denen Dora (about 3.2 Ma): Homotherium and Crocuta venustula.
- Masses [O, Wikipedia]: Megantereon whitei (Africa) 66.6 kg, other populations 100-120 kg; Homotherium up to 200-250 kg; Dinofelis 149 kg (one study); Pachycrocuta average 100-110 kg, very large 150 kg; Chasmaporthetes: only the North American species has a mass (99 kg). Megantereon in Kenya (South Turkwel) 3-3.5 Ma. Draft ranges NOT matched: Dinofelis 70-120, Pachycrocuta 100-190.
- Earliest leopard about 2 Ma, East Africa; earliest lion about 2 Ma, Olduvai [O, Wikipedia: needs a primary source]; no lion record found in the 2.5 Ma bin.
- Densities: leopard Serengeti 5.72 wet and 5.41 dry per 100 km2 [O] (phys.org/news/2020-08-serengeti-leopard-population-densities-healthy.html), range across Africa 0.1-30.3 [S]; spotted hyena Ruaha 3.55-10.80 per 100 km2 [O] (ora.ox.ac.uk), historic Ngorongoro 143 [S]; lion and wild dog [S] only (not usable).
- Life history [O]: spotted hyena gestation 110 d, litter 1-3 (usually 1-2), interbirth about 18 months, first birth 2-5 y, wild cub death 40-50% in year one, adult death 13-15% a year in protected areas. Leopard gestation 90-106 d, litter usually 2, interbirth 14-39 months, first birth average 46 months (33-62), wild lifespan poorly known (ielc.libguides.com fact sheets). Wikipedia's leopard figures disagree with the field data; the field data are used.
- Hominin predation: SK 54 punctures line up with leopard canines [O] (discovermagazine.com) but one study disputes it [S]; Dinofelis as "specialist primate killer" (Brain 1981) [S]; Megantereon isotopes suggest hominin and baboon prey at Swartkrans [O, Wikipedia only]; Taung eagle and Olduvai crocodile disputed [S]. NO rate found.
**NOT FOUND ONLINE:** a per-species guild list for the two time bins; any carnivoran list for Gona or Ledi-Geraru; Shungura C-F and Koobi Fora lists; Lycaon, Pachycrocuta and Megantereon at Hadar; African Chasmaporthetes mass; lion, wild dog and cheetah densities (beyond snippets); a primary source for the earliest lion; wild lifespan of leopard and hyena; any hominin predation rate.
