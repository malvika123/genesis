# Known gaps in Genesis, in plain words (started 23 Nov 2026 at the owner's request: "we put important things for later and then forget")

A gap is something real that our simulated hominins don't have yet, or have wrong. Each line says what is missing, why it matters, and where it stands. Kept up to date at every milestone and shown to the owner. (The long technical list is `docs/BACKLOG.md`.)

**Building now**
- **Strength and a real body (muscle, fat, protein).** Hominins have no strength; every one is equally frail; a fight is a dice roll. Real apes are strong, and strength depends on daily effort and food. Needs protein in food, a muscle and fat state, and a strength ceiling that runs in families. *Evidence and design in progress.*
- **Escaping an attack.** An attacked hominin used to die at once. A first change gives it one moment to get away (tested: about a quarter fewer predator deaths, but mostly because hunters let go, not because victims break free: too forgiving, kept off for now). Still to come: climbing to escape, group rescue, alarm calls, watching for danger, choosing a safe sleeping place.

**Next**
- **Insects and leaves in the diet (the owner, 23 Nov).** Living apes eat young leaves, termites and ants, and some meat; our hominins get about 96% of their protein from fruit, almost none from leaves, and there are no insects at all. Real protein sources are missing, so our hominins are probably protein-poor and unlike a chimpanzee. Evidence first (what wild apes and early hominins ate: leaves, termites, ants, honey, eggs), then build. (Backlog A29 and A15 had insects as "not yet built".)
- **A realistic place to live (the valley).** The globe's map is too coarse to hold several separate bands. The valley is built but its rules still count in "squares" (mating, attack reach, sight, food, hunters per area). Today the valley is too safe and has half the births.
- **Several bands that last, with young females moving between them,** the way real ape populations survive.
- **Food that is realistic per area** (kilos per hectare) and **hunters at real numbers per area.**
- **Missing predators:** *Homotherium*, *Dinofelis* and the giant hyena were there (lions were not).

**Waiting (known, not forgotten)**
- Bones, breaks and healing; limb lengths; running and walking efficiency (the rest of the body model).
- Water refuges: springs and groundwater that last through dry seasons.
- Thunderstorms and fire in the valley (it has none yet); fire-making is frozen on purpose.
- A real-length year (ours is squeezed into 3 days).
- Hominins keep entering deep water they can't swim in; real apes avoid it.
- Meat is eaten about three times too fast.
- Brains: whether a bigger brain can ever pay off; two decisions about the learner (a size rule and short-term memory) are made but not yet tested together; a harder test of "does a bigger brain help" is ready to run.
- Grooming, play, comforting and making up after a fight are not in the simulation.
- The age mothers have their first baby looks too high; mothers nurse about half as long as wild chimpanzees.
- Whether several kinds of hominin lived together in the valley's region (unverified).

**Things that were wrong and are fixed or corrected** (so we remember): the chain test was confounded; the poison bug that slow eating exposed; the earlier claim that several bands help (it was only a fixed number of predators); the claim that the valley population was level (it rises then falls).

**A rule for every item above:** each new thing is built with its view on the page in the same step (what a hominin's panel shows, the log lines, the picture), because the page is the owner's source of truth.
