# Colour eye and curiosity: the smallest honest build — Opus's design from the code, 23 Nov 2026 (branch `diet`); evidence `docs/reviews/2026-11-23-colour-vision-and-salience-sources.md` and `2026-11-23-primate-food-curiosity-sources.md`; part of `docs/design/realistic-diet.md`

**Verdict (Opus): yes, it can be built honestly without adding a single brain input.** Turn the poison-only "red berries" sense into a general "reddish thing stands out" sense; put "leaves within reach" into input slots that sit unused unless raw-vision mode is on; add two curiosity pulls that are inborn but fade with use; **do not put colour into the "food" sense.**
**The eye today (`src/senses.js` 215–308):** 8 directions × 16 channels (water, food, danger, herbivore, person, predator, mate, family, fish, red berries, sea, steep climb, trees, stones/sticks, nuts, fire). Food (1) is a label of meaning, not looks: the strongest of ripe fruit × 1.4, cooked food × 1.6, roots × 0.6 (270–280). **Red berries (9) fires only on poison squares, and only for eyes with three colour cones (293): red always means poison: a hidden label.** Trees (12) is `cover()` (trees, brush and caves together: `forest.js` 101). **Leaves are not strictly invisible** (leafy squares show up as "trees"), but nothing tells the brain it is standing among leaves (sight offsets skip its own square, `state.js` 142–150; the underfoot senses HB 364–375, FOODIN 397, IB+16 at 402 leave leaves out), young leaves, flowers and unripe fruit do not exist in the world, and ripe fruit never looks red, so it never looks like the poison berries. **Why colour must not go into channel 1:** two inborn things read it: an inborn weight of 3.5 (`brain.js` 159) and the hungry seek pull (`sim.js` 444): it would make an inborn liking for a colour, which nothing supports.
**Honest build `--colour-eye` (no input added; none of these slots has an inborn weight):** channel 9 becomes "something reddish, orange or yellow standing out against green, within sight" (ripe fruit `fruit[i]`; the young-leaf flush once diet step b exists, scaled by a registered share 0.3–1 of young leaves reddish enough to stand out; the poison berries), still limited to three-cone eyes; IB+16 becomes the same signal for the creature's own square; one unused raw-vision slot (`RET`, `config.js` 32) carries "leaves and branches within reach" = `cover(here)`; `--colour-eye` and `--retina` cannot run together. **Signal:** ripe fruit high; reddish young leaves medium (the registered share); poison berries high, **the same as fruit** (telling them apart needs tasting, food memory or the food sense, which still includes fruit); mature leaves, grass, bare ground, water 0; flowers and unripe fruit nothing (not in the world: do not add them just so they can be seen); predators and prey unchanged; brightness and movement left out (no motion sense). **Labels that remain** (each one's removal changes every world: its own later step from Fable's 17 Nov list): food (1); danger (lumps rocks, pits, deep water); sea versus lake; person, herbivore and predator categories (softened by learned recognition, `perceive` 93–121); mate (reads hidden breeding state, all-round at 6 × range, 311–323); family (by identity, 305). Water can stay.
**Curiosity today:** a place-novelty reward and faster learning after something new (`a.vis`, `a.novel`, `bonus[6]`, `sim.js` 227–237, 558, 575) and the curiosity feeling input IB+8 (`senses.js` 422). **Bug (to report, not fix silently): `sim.js` 233 sets `a.bored = 0` before 236 uses it, so `(1 + 2 * a.bored)` is always 1.**
**(i) `--taste-curious K` (registered range, 0 = today):** a pull added to `RF[A_EAT]` where something is within reach (IB+16, the leaf slot, roots): strength = K × `g.c` × youth × (1 − caution) × (1 − fear) × mother boost; youth falls to a low adult level by about 4–8 years (mapped onto `S.mat`); caution (fear of new food) rises with age and fades with each safe taste of that food kind (`a.tasted[kind]`; fade length 8–15 tastes, tested at both ends); sickness resets caution at once (`sim.js` 966); hunger: no link or a tiny one (the hungry try-plants pull, 348, stays); mother boost larger if the mother is in the same square and visibly ate (`visAct`/`visRes`, 566–568), smaller for other group-mates. "Kind" is a world category that feeds only this inborn machinery (as `a.vis` already does), never the network: taste and smell do tell foods apart. **(ii) `--novelty-approach K2`:** a pull toward directions where channel 9, 13 (stones, sticks) or 14 (nuts) shows something the individual is little used to; a habituation `a.hab[c]` rises with exposure (the orienting response that fades with repetition); same scaling; **never** on danger, predators or fire (they keep their fear pulls). **Rules for both:** no pull to any particular food or colour; no reward for tasting (the gut, the existing reward and copying teach); `a.novel = tick` on a first taste reuses the learning boost.
**Steps (each behind a switch; prediction first; off = identical worlds):** (a) measure only (diet step a plus a colour log: how often hungry hominins see a reddish patch, stand among leaves, and eat; prediction: leaves under 2% of feeding time); (b) `--colour-eye` (prediction: a small change; berry sickness rises somewhat because fruit and berries now look alike; leaves under 3% because nothing pulls toward them yet; check berry-sickness count and hunger deaths at both ends of the young-leaf share); (c) the young-leaf crop (diet b) on the colour sense, **not** on channel 1 or HB (HB carries an inborn eat weight of 4, `brain.js` about 107) (leaves 5–15% of feeding time, more in lean months); (d) `--taste-curious` at K = 0, low and high (the young widen their diets first; adult protein about 1.0 → 1.1–1.3 g/kg); (e) `--novelty-approach`. **Checks after each:** leaves 5–25% of feeding time and insects 1–5%; hunger deaths and births at both ends of every range (a large swing means the switch is setting the answer: report it, do not pick a value); the faithful-day table must not get worse. **Learned or wired:** the share of eating that comes from the network rather than the pulls (`mind()` already gets the pulls separately); leaf eating should persist in adults whose pull has faded, and in a run where the pull is switched off after year 5; diet should spread mother to child.
**The page (same step):** hominin panel: "Stands out to it: something reddish to the north-east (it cannot tell fruit from berries by sight)"; "Within reach: leaves and branches"; "Curious: 0.7; wary of new food: 0.3; tasted leaves 2 times"; "Pulled by curiosity (inborn, fading): +0.4 toward eating", shown apart from what its network chose. Wording to change: channel 9 in `mind.js` CHN (22) and INNER[16] (47) becomes "something reddish standing out"; fix the whispers text (`whispers.js` 133–134) and the lessons text (`lessons.js` 8–9), which call it "red berries". Map: tint the reddish squares this individual sees (`a.view`), mark the square it is investigating, log first tastes.
**Risks / would not:** a new brain input would change every world (slot reuse avoids it); do not tune K, the young-leaf share or the fade length toward a target share; one bite must not exceed the sourced kcal per minute; "lean months" are 24 steps; the reused raw-vision slots carry inherited random weights (honest, unlearned wiring: expect some noise at first); would not put colour or leaves on channel 1 or HB, add any colour liking, flowers or unripe fruit, reward novelty itself, or use it alongside `--retina`.
**Owner decides:** (1) whether to strip fruit from the food label and drop the hungry pull toward it later (the big honest-eye step, measured on its own); (2) the mother-boost size; (3) whether to fold brightness into channel 9. **Could not verify (Opus):** that red-green pop-out is innate and automatic; that young primates approach new objects more (Glickman & Sroges 1966 exists; age result second-hand); the share of African young leaves that are red; the 8–15-taste figure in apes.
