# Hands with fingers (plan item 34) — 24 Nov 2026

**Owner:** give them fingers and make them work like real fingers.
**Step 1, built (drawing only, no effect on the world).** In the first-person view each hand now has four long curved fingers and a short thumb; fingers curl when something is held. Not yet on the 2D figures or the 3D figures seen from outside.
**Evidence (live search 24 Nov).**
- Thumb: Alba et al. 2003 (Hadar AL 333): a fully human thumb-to-hand ratio, a pad-to-pad pinch possible. Rolian et al. 2013: the earlier assignment of bones to one individual is questionable; proportions between gorilla and human; no human-efficiency precision grip. Feix et al. 2015 (composite hand): human-like precision manipulation plausible. Marzke 1997: limited thumb mobility compromises toolmaking; the hand may have gripped well between the thumb and the side of the second finger (small flakes, wooden probes).
- Fingers: curved, a climbing hand (and the curve is also seen in Homo naledi); how far curvature limited handling is not settled.
**Drawn as:** curved fingers, thumb shorter than human (between the two readings).
**Step 2, not built (a test, not a gift).** A grip trait (power clasp plus a limited pinch) deciding how well small flakes and sticks are handled; registered as an assumption and run at both ends (Rolian's intermediate hand and Alba's human-like hand). The brain is not touched; nothing is handed over.
