# Assumptions register

Every number in Genesis should come from research, or be listed here as an assumption with the range the
research allows. **Nothing is tuned toward an outcome**: uncertain numbers are tested at both ends of their
range, and results that depend on them say so (see `docs/FINDINGS.md`).

Status of each entry: **sourced** (the number is from research), **direction** (research gives the direction
or a range; the number is our choice within it), **assumption** (no research number exists; our choice),
**deviation** (we knowingly depart from the evidence, and why).

Started 10 November 2026; covers the early-hominin world and the mechanisms added in November 2026. The
older parts of the model are still to be audited the same way.

## The two clocks (decided by the owner, 14 Nov 2026; Fable's scale review, finding 3)

A year is squeezed into 3 simulated days (`TY` = 288 steps), but each day keeps a real day's 24 hours (`DAY` = 96 steps of 15
minutes). **Everything a body does in a day is on the day clock, as in real life**: walking, eating and drinking, sleeping, a hunter's
daily food need (leopard 3.5–4.9 kg, hyena 3.8–4.0 kg a real day), how fast a carcass rots, how far animals range. **Everything about
a life is on the life clock**: growing up, ageing, pregnancy, nursing, breeding, lifespans. The cost, said plainly: a year holds only
3 days of food, births and deaths, so whether food over a year can support a population is not true to life (the squeezed year is
listed among the simplifications). Rejected: putting daily needs on the life clock (a hunter would need about 490 kg a simulated day).

## Early hominins, about 2.5 million years ago (`HOMININ` in `src/config.js`)

**Which hominin Genesis stands for** (realism audit, gap 9; written 14 Nov 2026): not one species but **a generic early hominin of
East Africa about 2.6–2.5 million years ago**, at the time of the first Oldowan tools (Gona, Ledi-Geraru, Nyayanga) and the
contemporaries *Australopithecus garhi*, *Paranthropus aethiopicus* and the earliest *Homo* (the Ledi-Geraru jaw, about 2.8 Ma).
Where these differ, Genesis takes the australopith-sized body (adults about 30 / 42 kg), chimpanzee-like growth and life history
(teeth say ape-like timing), and early-*Homo*-like nursing (about 4 years), each switchable; results say so.

## The animals of East Africa about 2.5 million years ago (`HKINDS` in `src/config.js`; patch 6)

**The default since 14 Nov 2026** (realism audit, gap 1; `--ice-animals` restores the ice-age animals). In a 4-world, 30-day check all
three hunter kinds survived (the newcomer rule, entry 39) and hyenas and leopards killed hominins. **Still a known deviation:** about nine
times too many hunters for the prey (Carbone & Gittleman 2002; entry 24) — the scale step. **Still missing:** *Dinofelis* and
*Homotherium* (other big cats of the time), crocodiles, and eagles (the Taung child: Berger & Clarke 1995).

Each replaces an ice-age kind in the hominin world with `--africa-animals` — **off by default** until hunters and prey are balanced (research log entry 24). Life histories from the living
species; numbers inside the ranges given. Abundance (`cap`, `share`) and the model's own settings (speed, size relative
to a deer or wolf) are carried over from the kinds they replace: **assumptions**. Temperature limits and habitat ranges
(warm lands) are **direction**.
